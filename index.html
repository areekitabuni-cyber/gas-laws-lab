Important unit rule

Always use absolute temperature in kelvin (K). Convert °C to K by adding 273.15. Keep pressure units consistent on both sides and volume units consistent on both sides. For the ideal gas law, use R = 8.314 kPa·L/(mol·K) when pressure is in kPa and volume in L, or R = 0.082057 L·atm/(mol·K) when pressure is in atm.

Formula guide

Boyle: P₁V₁ = P₂V₂

Constant temperature and fixed amount of gas.

Charles: V₁/T₁ = V₂/T₂

Constant pressure and fixed amount of gas.

Gay-Lussac: P₁/T₁ = P₂/T₂

Constant volume and fixed amount of gas.

Combined: P₁V₁/T₁ = P₂V₂/T₂

Same gas sample between two equilibrium states.

Ideal gas: PV = nRT

P = pressure, V = volume, n = amount in moles, R = gas constant, T = kelvin temperature.

How to solve problems

  1. Write down the known values and identify the unknown.
  2. Convert temperatures to kelvin.
  3. Choose the correct law and rearrange it for the unknown.
  4. Substitute values with consistent units.
  5. Check whether the answer makes physical sense.
